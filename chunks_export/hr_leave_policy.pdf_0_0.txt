Acme Corp - Leave Policy 1. Overview This leave policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Corp. It describes the entitlements, request procedures, and approval workflow for annual leave, sick leave, and other categories of time off. 2. Annual Leave Entitlement Full-time employees are entitled to 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the following year and must be consumed by 31 March; any remaining balance is forfeit. 3. Sick Leave Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a licensed practitioner. Sick leave balances do not carry over. 4. Leave Request Process All leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the intended start date. Emergency leave may be requested retroactively within 24 hours of returning to work. Line managers are expected to approve or decline a request within 2 working days. 5. Unpaid Leave Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted at the discretion of the line manager after annual leave has been exhausted. Requests longer than 30 days require approval from the HR Director. 6. Public Holidays Acme Corp observes 12 public holidays per year. A full calendar is published every December. Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to either a day in lieu or a 150% pay uplift.
