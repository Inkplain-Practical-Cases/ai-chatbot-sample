Acme Corp - Code of Conduct 1. Purpose The Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of every employee, contractor, and representative of Acme Corp. It is intended to promote a safe, respectful, and professional workplace. 2. Respectful Workplace Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is not tolerated. This includes unwanted physical contact, offensive language, or conduct that creates a hostile environment based on gender, race, religion, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic. 3. Conflicts of Interest Employees must disclose any actual or perceived conflict of interest to their line manager in writing. Examples include directorships in external companies, close family relationships with suppliers, or financial holdings in competitors. 4. Confidentiality All employees are bound by confidentiality obligations that survive termination of employment. Customer data, financial information, and strategic plans must not be shared outside the company without prior written authorisation from the relevant department head. 5. Social Media Employees may maintain personal social media accounts but must not represent themselves as speaking on behalf of Acme Corp unless expressly authorised. Posts that disclose confidential information or bring the company into disrepute may result in disciplinary action. 6. Reporting Violations Suspected violations should be reported through the confidential whistleblower hotline or directly to the HR Director. Retaliation against a person making a good-faith report is itself a violation of this Code.
