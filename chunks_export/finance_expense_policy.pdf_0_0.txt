Acme Corp - Expense Policy 1. Introduction The expense policy describes how employees may incur and reclaim business expenses. It applies to all employees and approved contractors acting on behalf of Acme Corp. 2. Pre-Approval Expenses above USD 500 require pre-approval from the line manager. Expenses above USD 2,500 additionally require approval from the department Finance Partner. Travel bookings should be made through the corporate travel portal wherever possible. 3. Meals and Entertainment Meals while travelling on company business are reimbursed up to a per-diem of USD 75 per day within North America and Europe, and USD 50 per day elsewhere. Client entertainment above USD 150 per attendee must be pre-approved. 4. Ground Transport Taxis, ride-share, and short-term car rentals are reimbursable when used for legitimate business travel. Daily commuting between home and the employee's primary office is not reimbursable. 5. Non-Reimbursable Items The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances: alcoholic drinks not accompanying a meal, personal entertainment, fines and traffic tickets, spa or beauty treatments, and personal phone upgrades. 6. Submission Deadline Expense claims must be submitted with itemised receipts within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically and require a written exception from the Finance Director to be considered.
