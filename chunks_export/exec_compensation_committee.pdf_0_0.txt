Acme Corp - Compensation Committee Charter 1. Purpose The Compensation Committee reviews and approves executive pay, long-term incentive design, and annual bonus pools. This document summarises the committee's charter, meeting cadence, and approval thresholds. 2. Membership The committee comprises four independent non-executive directors. The Chair is elected annually. The Chief People Officer and the Chief Financial Officer attend as non-voting advisors. External remuneration consultants may be engaged as required. 3. Meeting Cadence The committee meets at least four times per year. Special sessions may be convened by the Chair to address matters arising from an acquisition, restructuring, or material change in strategy. Quorum is three members. 4. Executive Pay Review Executive base pay is benchmarked against a peer group of 18 comparable companies. The 2026 peer group composition was finalised in the Q3 2025 meeting. Target total compensation is positioned at the 60th percentile with upside to the 75th percentile subject to performance. 5. Long-Term Incentives Long-term incentives consist of restricted stock units (50%) and performance share units (50%). The PSU vesting is tied to a combination of relative total shareholder return and free cash flow growth measured over a three-year cycle. 6. Clawback Incentive awards are subject to clawback in the event of material financial restatement, executive misconduct, or breach of post-termination restrictions. The clawback window is three years from the date of vesting.
