Acme Corp - Travel & Reimbursement Policy 1. Booking Travel All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal managed by the approved travel provider. Direct bookings with airlines, hotels, or third-party aggregators are not reimbursable except in documented emergencies. 2. Flight Class Entitlement Flights of under 6 hours are economy class for all grades. Flights of 6 to 10 hours allow premium economy for Grade 4 and above. Flights exceeding 10 hours allow business class for Grade 5 and above. Upgrades using personal miles are always permitted. 3. Hotel Standards Hotels must fall within the nightly rate cap of USD 250 for designated high-cost cities and USD 180 elsewhere. Employees may stay at higher-rated hotels only when the preferred hotel is unavailable or when attending a company-organised conference at a specific venue. 4. Per Diem In addition to actual meal receipts, travellers may elect a flat per-diem of USD 60 per full day of travel, reduced to USD 30 for travel days where departure or arrival occurs after 14:00 local. 5. Travel Insurance Business travel is automatically covered by the corporate travel insurance policy. Employees must register the trip in the travel portal for coverage to apply. Purchase of separate travel insurance is not reimbursable. 6. Non-Employee Travel Travel expenses for candidates, contractors, or family members are not reimbursed under this policy. A separate guest-travel form is required, subject to approval from the Chief People Officer.
