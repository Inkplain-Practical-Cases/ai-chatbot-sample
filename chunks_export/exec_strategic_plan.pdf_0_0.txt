Strategic Plan 2026-2028 (Scanned Acme Strategic Plan 2026 to 2028. This document summarises the three-year strategic priorities agreed by the Executive Committee in the January 2026 offsite. Priority 1: Accelerate international expansion in the APAC region: The plan targets a doubling of APAC revenue by the of 2027, with a particular focus on Singapore, and Australia. Investment of approximately USD 45 million is earmarked for this priority, split across sales capacity, localisation, and a new regional distribution hub in Singapore Priority 2: Shift the product portfolio toward recurring revenue. The target is for subscription revenue to represent 65 percent of total revenue by the end of 2028,up 38 percent at the close of 2025. This will be supported by a managed transition programme for existing perpetual-license customers and a launch of two new subscription tiers in Q2 2026. Priority 3: Reduce unit cost of delivery by 1S percent over the plan period. Initiatives include a consolidation of the cloud footprint onto a primary vendor; retirement of three products, and migration of back-office systems to single ERP instance by the end of 2027. Priority 4: Build an Al-first operating model. Every core business process will be reviewed for automation opportunities. A central AI Centre of Excellence will be formed in Q1 2026 and funded through a three-year programme of USD 25 million Early focus areas are customer support; finance reconciliations, and sales pipeline qualification Board approval for this plan was secured on 15 February 2026. Quarterly progress reviews will be presented to the Board by the Chief Executive Officer.
