Acme Corp - Performance Review Process (Manager Only) 1. Scope This document describes the performance review process and is intended for use by line managers and above. It contains guidance on calibration, rating distribution, and compensation linkage. 2. Review Cycle Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full review in December. Each review includes self-assessment, manager assessment, and a calibration step with peer managers. 3. Rating Scale Employees are rated on a five-point scale: 1 Below Expectations, 2 Developing, 3 Meets Expectations, 4 Exceeds Expectations, 5 Outstanding. The expected distribution across a team is roughly 10/20/50/15/5. Calibration sessions ensure fairness across teams. 4. Compensation Linkage Year-end ratings drive the annual merit increase and bonus multiplier. The merit budget is 4% on average. A rating of 3 typically yields a merit increase of 3 to 4%, a rating of 4 yields 5 to 7%, and a rating of 5 yields 8 to 12% plus an accelerated equity refresh. 5. Performance Improvement Plans An employee rated 1 or receiving two consecutive ratings of 2 will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 to 90 days. HR Business Partners must be engaged at the outset of any PIP. 6. Documentation Managers must document specific examples supporting each rating. Vague statements such as 'strong performer' are not acceptable in the calibration discussion. Examples must reference observable outcomes, deadlines met or missed, and customer impact.
